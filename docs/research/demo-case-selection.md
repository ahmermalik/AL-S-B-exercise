# Demonstration case selection review

Reviewed October 31, 2026 after the user challenged whether Campus Edge was the strongest case for demonstrating programming and AI capability. An independent subagent and direct workbook inspection compared alternatives. The user subsequently selected Sunset Ridge for the revised walkthrough (D010) and accepted the revised conceptual Screen 3 (D012). Supporting cases remain proposed.

## Conclusion

Campus Edge is useful for teaching evidence requests, honest abstention, and commentary revision. It is a weaker flagship because the payroll explanation is absent and its interesting revision needs additional simulated material.

Sunset Ridge is the selected lead demonstration. Aggie Commons for material duplicate review and Campus Edge for missing-evidence/revision behavior remain proposed supporting examples (P017). Preserve one shared Case review structure rather than build separate applications for the examples.

## Engineering and AI boundaries

Code normalizes invoice identifiers, compares structured keys, calculates amounts, and enforces version/decision state. The agent retrieves the relevant invoice, account, property, and tracker records. The LLM assists with description/account interpretation, explains the evidence, drafts specific follow-ups, and qualifies recommendations. A fixed synthetic description can also be mapped by ordinary rules; the demonstration must not pretend a lookup requires an LLM.

Meaningful AI benefit becomes clearer with varied descriptions and attributable operator context, tested against a labelled evaluation set. The choice of case alone does not establish useful model behavior.

The manager reviews proposals. Do not silently post corrections, change budgets, subtract candidate invoices from B3 actuals, or imply that all AP records are posted GL entries. Accounting-system integration and capitalization/coding policy are not supplied.

## Candidate rejected as a causal explanation

Copper Canyon has R&M actual 19,090 versus budget 11,570, but supplied R&M AP rows total only 5,577.92. Its flooring records are already capital-coded. That evidence cannot establish a root-cause explanation for the variance.
